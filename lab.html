Common Room Lab

Up to six people share a kitchen-lounge, chat, and get on with everyday jobs. Give each of them a different mix of perceptual, motor and cognitive difficulties, then watch how their routes, conversations and plans change. Or play Brain detective: spot which part of the brain is behind someone's hidden difficulty.

    How the model works

    Every rule below runs locally in this page

    These are deliberately simple teaching models built from rules and dice rolls. They are not clinical descriptions of any condition. Real people adapt, use strategies, and vary from day to day. The point is to see how a single difficulty ripples through an ordinary task, and how small changes to the room can soften it.
